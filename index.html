About Us

Who We Are

Founded by CEO Marclesio Teixeira, MTX General Construction was created with a purpose far beyond construction — carrying forward a family legacy built on hard work, honesty and dedication. The name “MTX” comes from the initials of Marclesio and the “TX” from Teixeira, honoring his father’s legacy and values.

With over 10 years of experience serving the Boston region, MTX has become known for high-quality craftsmanship, fair pricing and projects completed on schedule. From renovations to custom construction, our mission is to provide reliable service and create spaces that exceed expectations.

Today, MTX continues to grow as a solid and trusted company, earning the satisfaction and confidence of clients and friends through professionalism, commitment and attention to every detail.
